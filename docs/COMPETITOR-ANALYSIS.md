## Observations
- The market clusters into: caption polish, long→short clipping, transcript editing, general browser editors, mobile editors, and a few "all-in-one" claims [1][3][6].
- Source evidence shows little about **intent-first workflow generation**, **cross-asset dependency tracking**, or **change impact analysis**; these are therefore our hypothesis for differentiation, not a proven white space.

## CreatorAI Differentiation
1. **Intent → dynamic workflow** instead of tool menus.
2. **AI Creative Director** (pre-production shot plans) for people without footage.
3. **Content Genome + Impact Propagation** (what depends on what; what to update after a change).
4. **Content Archaeology** (mine unused assets) and **Creator DNA** (personalization).
5. **Reference DNA** (structure-based inspiration with safe boundaries).
6. **Editable AI output** (EDL, versions, human review).

Positioning: "Most creator AI tools help you generate or edit content. CreatorAI understands what you are trying to create and orchestrates the entire creative workflow."

## Caveats
Competitors may have unlisted features; pricing/features change; sources are comparison blogs with possible bias (several are published by competing vendors, e.g., Submagic, Jupitrr, Typito). Revalidate before pitching claims as fact.
